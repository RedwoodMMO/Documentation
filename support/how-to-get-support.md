# How to get Support

Before reaching out, check the [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md) page; it covers the most common setup and integration issues.

Standard License users can leverage our community-driven support channel in our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/Gj23MHhCQR). Redwood developers periodically monitor these channels, especially to look out for bugs, and may answer your questions.

Need private and/or dedicated support from Redwood developers? We have [custom licenses](https://redwoodmultiplayer.com/custom-licenses) available that can include extra support. [Contact us](https://redwoodmultiplayer.com/contact) to get a customized quote!

## Reporting Bugs

When you report a bug, please include:

- The Redwood backend and Unreal plugin versions (they should match), your Unreal Engine version, and your OS
- How you're running the backend (Dev Initiator or Kubernetes) and which game server provider your realm uses
- The relevant logs: the backend output and the `LogRedwood` lines from the client and/or game server logs (see [Where to Find Logs](./troubleshooting.md#where-to-find-logs))
- Your config environment with any secrets removed (`yarn cli get-config <env>` prints the merged configuration)
- Reproduction steps

## Schedule a Call

If you need to schedule a call, please send us an email at [redwood@incanta.dev](mailto:redwood@incanta.dev) and we'll find a time that works for you!
